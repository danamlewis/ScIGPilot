Interpretation notes

Model profiles and bands: Profiles are editable assumptions, not treatment targets. Shaded bands reflect baseline, slope, absorption, and elimination assumptions—not individual prediction intervals.

Reference lines: Lab ranges are orientation only and are not interpreted as good or bad for someone receiving SCIG.

Product handling: Requested doses are rounded to feasible cartridge combinations; cartridge counts must match before downstream outputs are shown.

Comparisons: Relative exposure is normalized to the reference average. Sites, mL/site, cartridges, and infusion time describe feasibility and burden, not measured serum concentration.

Scope: This simulator does not provide clinical recommendations, patient-specific fitting, risk prediction, accounts, or backend storage.
