SCIG Schedule Simulator

Compare relative exposure shape, model-derived IgG amplitude, dose intensity, infusion-site burden, and estimated infusion time across subcutaneous immunoglobulin schedules.

This is a comparative simulator. It estimates model-derived IgG ranges from editable assumptions, not an individual patient's true serum IgG level, and it does not recommend dose changes.

Interpretation Notes

How to read the simulator outputs and current assumptions.

Scenario presets

IgG presets are editable starting assumptions. They scale the estimated IgG bands but are not treatment targets.

Reference range

Lab reference lines are shown for orientation against a typical non-SCIG range; they are not interpreted as good or bad for someone receiving SCIG.

Product rounding

The requested protocol dose is converted to product volume and rounded to a feasible cartridge combination. The dose basis remains the entered protocol value.

Cartridges

Conventional SCIG product selection changes concentration and presentation sizes. Cartridge counts must match the generated dose before downstream outputs are shown.

Exposure model

Relative exposure is normalized to the reference regimen average. It is a model comparison, not a measured serum concentration.

Burden metrics

Sites, mL/site, cartridge counts, and infusion time are feasibility and burden variables. They do not alter systemic exposure in this version.

Uncertainty bands

Shaded IgG bands reflect the editable baseline, slope, absorption, and elimination assumptions. They are not individual prediction intervals.

Scope

This simulator does not provide clinical recommendations, dose-adjustment advice, patient-specific fitting, risk prediction, accounts, or backend storage.
